The Complete Yoga Teacher's Manual

Not a collection of poses. Not a curriculum outline. Yoga as a living tradition — philosophy, physiology, and the part that only surfaces after a decade of teaching — set down whole.

02  The workshops

Modern Yogi Workshops

A series for teachers who finished their training and discovered it was the beginning. Held in the studios where Atma Yoga is already taught.

03  The author

Atma

He was ten years old, in the company of yogis and swamis. Seventeen teachers followed, seven of them sanyasi. Yoga Alliance E-RYT 500, with more than 10,000 hours spent training teachers across Europe, Asia and North America.

He has spent a lifetime learning this. The book is the shortest way he knows to hand it over.
